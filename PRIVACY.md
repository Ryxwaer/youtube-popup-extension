# Privacy Policy – PiP Anywhere

_Last updated: November 2, 2026_

PiP Anywhere does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension accesses

- **YouTube player state.** On youtube.com, the extension reads the playing video's
  state (play/pause, current time, duration, size, title) and its video frames, so it
  can show them in the floating window on your other tabs.
- **Tabs.** It checks which tab is active so it knows when to show or hide the
  floating window.
- **Pages you visit.** It adds the floating window to the page you're on. It doesn't
  read or modify that page's content.

## Where the data goes

Nowhere outside your browser. The video frames are sent from the YouTube tab to the
tab showing the floating window over a WebRTC connection between two tabs of your own
browser, with no servers involved. The extension makes no network requests of its own
and contains no analytics or tracking.

## What is stored

Only the floating window's last position and size, kept in the browser's local
extension storage so it reappears where you left it, plus temporary session state that
is cleared when the browser closes. Uninstalling the extension removes both.
