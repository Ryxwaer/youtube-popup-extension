PiP Anywhere

Your YouTube video, floating on every tab.

Sizes

Pinch or double-tap the window to switch between these.

How to use

  • Play, then switch tabsThe video follows you to every tab.
  • Drag it anywhereFlick it and it glides to an edge.
  • Pinch to resizeOr double-tap it. Double-click works too.
  • Tuck it awayPush it into the edge. Pull the tab to bring it back.
  • Tap for controlsPlay, pause, skip, or go back to YouTube.

No account, no tracking. The video never leaves your browser.
